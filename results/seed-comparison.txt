Two seeds of the same experiment, compared like for like.

novalue11 (seed 1234) and novalue11_seed5678 are independent training runs of the
same agent on the same task. Each was fine-tuned onto the same grid of values and
analysed with the same scripts. Caveat on the arms: seed 1234's sweeps ran 3M
fine-tuning steps, seed 5678's 750k, so anything scaling with fine-tune length is
not comparable between them. Seed 1234's own 750k numbers are given where that
matters.

=== what replicates ===

  cos(axis_0, axis_1), disattenuated by split-half reliability
    seed 1234, 3M arms     raw -0.255   ceilings 0.262/0.252   ->  -0.992
    seed 1234, 750k arms   raw -0.353   ceilings 0.382/0.371   ->  -0.937
    seed 5678, 750k arms   raw -0.428   ceilings 0.424/0.452   ->  -0.978

  One knob, in both agents. The raw cosines differ threefold and mean nothing on
  their own; they are attenuated by how noisy each axis estimate is, and the
  ceiling correction is what makes them comparable.

  exchange rate, fitted across the fine-tuned arms
    seed 1234   -15.21 extra steps per unit of value
    seed 5678   -15.63
    task-optimal would be -20.0

  Both agents undercharge for distance by the same ~22%, which is a fact about
  what the task teaches rather than about either set of weights.

=== what does not replicate ===

  where the agent sits when the values are equal
    seed 1234   7.7 extra steps   implied step penalty 0.065 against a true 0.05
    seed 5678   8.5 extra steps   implied step penalty 0.059

  Same slope, different intercept. Each agent settles at its own indifference
  point and then trades at the same rate away from it.

  which channels carry the value  (cell_list_0/ih, enrichment over the shared
  fine-tuning component)
    seed 1234   ch07 2.29  ch01 2.21  | then a gap to ch12 1.31
    seed 5678   ch15 1.75  ch04 1.57  ch25 1.54  ch16 1.50 | no gap

  top-8 overlap ACROSS SEEDS: 3 of 8, against 2.0 by chance -- i.e. nothing.
  Neither of seed 1234's two leaders appears anywhere in seed 5678's top eight.

  Within a seed the same measurement is highly repeatable. Two disjoint sweeps,
  one moving each objective, agree on the channel profile:
    seed 1234   top-8 overlap 7 of 8   profile r +0.81..+0.94   full fits +0.966
    seed 5678   top-8 overlap 6 of 8   profile r +0.53..+0.78   full fits +0.785

  So the localisation is real and reproducible inside one network, and carries no
  information about any other network trained the same way. "Channels 1 and 7"
  is a fact about novalue11, not about the architecture or the task.

=== writing the axis, each arm held out of the axis that predicts it ===

    value   seed 1234 err   seed 5678 err
     0.30       +0.1            +1.6
     0.40       +0.6            +1.0
     0.60       -0.4            +0.2
     0.70       -0.6            +0.2
     0.80       +0.1            +0.5
     0.90       +1.4            +1.1
    mean |err|   0.53            0.77
    signed mean +0.20           +0.77

  Writing works in both. On seed 5678 every error has the same sign, so its axis
  systematically overshoots rather than scattering -- worst at the ends of the
  grid, which is the signature of fitting a line to something slightly curved.
