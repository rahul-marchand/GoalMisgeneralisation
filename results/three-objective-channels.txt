  Every estimate here comes from one symmetric pair of arms, so across and
  within rest on the same number of arms and the ratio is a disattenuated
  cosine rather than a mixture of two sample sizes.

  With two objectives, one shared knob puts this at -1. With three it cannot:
  three vectors cannot be pairwise anti-parallel. There -0.5 is what a
  representation holding only differences gives, since three symmetric axes
  summing to zero must, and 0 is what three absolute registers give.


A handful of channels carrying the axis in both sweeps would be the first thing
here deserving the word circuit. Concentration in one sweep alone would not be:
a fitted axis is mostly sampling error, and noise concentrates by chance.
